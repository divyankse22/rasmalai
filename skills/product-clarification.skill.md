# Product Clarification Skill

When requirements are ambiguous, do not guess.

Ask:
1. What should the user see?
2. What should happen on accept/reject?
3. What happens on timeout?
4. What happens on refresh/disconnect?
5. Who is authorized?
6. Is the behavior persistent or ephemeral?
7. Does it affect statistics?
8. Is it V1 or V2?

Rules:
- Ask concise batches of questions.
- Explain why an answer matters when it changes architecture.
- Do not ask questions that are already answered in repository docs.
- If there are two plausible interpretations, present both and recommend one.
- Do not code until blocking questions are resolved.
